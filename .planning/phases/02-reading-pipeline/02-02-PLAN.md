## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 2 goal (verbatim): A local backend turns a palm photo, hand side and hand landmarks into a validated, safe, cached Palm / Love / Career reading through an async job API, proven on real palm photos before any client wiring.

Note (MVP mode): the Goal line is not in user-story format and no story was invented.

<objective>
Lines that fail validation against the hand landmarks are replaced by landmark-anchored template lines, and every line records which source drew it (READ-06, D-10). The eval's "template-first?" question gets its switch and its per-line pass data (D-11).

Purpose: the overlay must never draw a crease off the palm; trace_source is what lets the Phase 5/6 client label lines approximate.
Output: line_geometry.py, palm_reader wiring, tests.
</objective>
