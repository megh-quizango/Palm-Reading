## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 2 goal (verbatim): A local backend turns a palm photo, hand side and hand landmarks into a validated, safe, cached Palm / Love / Career reading through an async job API, proven on real palm photos before any client wiring.

Note (MVP mode): the Goal line is not in user-story format and no story was invented.

<objective>
Every way a model call can go wrong ends the job cleanly with one of the D-01 infrastructure codes, malformed output is retried exactly once (READ-02, D-05), refusals are not retried (D-04), and a retried POST returns the same job (D-02) while stale jobs expire (D-03).

Purpose: the Phase 6 client maps busy / timeout / model_error to its generating-screen states; it must never see a hung or half-finished job.
Output: claude_client error mapping, job retry and deadline policy, idempotency and single-flight, tests.
</objective>
