## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 3 goal (verbatim): A user can open the app, onboard once, land on a Home hub over the animated cosmic background, and pick which hand to scan, with everything persisted across restarts.

Note (MVP mode): the Goal line is not in user-story format and no story was invented. Run `/gsd-mvp-phase 3` if a user-story framing is wanted.

Note (wave 2): this plan expands the 03-01 Home from its header to the full hub and adds the hand choice behind it. It is the only wave-2 plan that edits AppNavDisplay.kt; 03-03 runs beside it on the onboarding package. Both share the phone through tools/device-lock.sh.

<objective>
A returning user lands on a Home hub (greeting, hero, 7-tile grid, coming-soon sheet, exit confirm) and goes from the hero to hand selection with photo tips and on to the scanner entry carrying the chosen hand, with the scan route opting out of the sky (HOME-01, HOME-02, HOME-04, SCAN-01, FND-03 opt-out; D-01, D-03, D-11, D-12, D-13, D-14, D-15).

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 3 criteria 3, 4 (opt-out half) and 5.
Output: Home screen parts, bottom-sheet scene strategy, NocturneTopBar, NocturneButton OnHero + press scale, FeatureCard and NocturneChip changes, hand selection and scan placeholder, strings_home.xml (en, hi), debug-only showcase, HomeTest and HandSelectTest.
</objective>
