## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 4 goal (verbatim): The user points the phone at a palm, gets stable live guidance, and captures or imports a photo that is processed and ready to upload, running smoothly on the reference low-end phone.

Note (MVP mode): the Goal line is not in user-story format and no story was invented. Run `/gsd-mvp-phase 4` if a user-story framing is wanted.

Note (scope): 23 files_modified entries (one is the fixture glob) across 3 tasks of 8-9 files each, accepted: each task is one concern with its own verify.

Note (wave 2): runs beside 04-03 (permission states) and 04-04 (processing) on disjoint files. This plan owns the live scanner: ScannerContent, ScannerViewModel and every file listed above. It needs no physical phone; thresholds stay at the research starting values until 04-05 calibrates them on the phone with both hands.

<objective>
Turn the tracer's raw palm/no-palm chip into stable live guidance (back of hand, closed hand, too far, moving, palm detected, wrong-hand hint) and give the scanner its real controls (500 ms hold before capture, focus-then-capture, tap-to-focus, torch), all driven by pure, fixture-tested logic (SCAN-03, SCAN-04; D-02, D-05, D-07, D-08, D-09 synthetic fixtures, D-16).

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 4 success criteria 2 (guidance reaches and holds each state, unit-tested against fixtures) and 3 (capture only with a palm, focus before capture, torch).
Output: HandGuidance + GuidanceConfig, GuidanceStabilizer, FillCenterMapping, the fixture generator and fixtures, GuidanceChip, PalmGuideOverlay, ShutterButton/CameraIconButton, ScanCamera, the upgraded ScannerViewModel and ScannerContent, and their tests.
</objective>
