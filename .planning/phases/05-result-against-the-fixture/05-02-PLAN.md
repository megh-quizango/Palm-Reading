## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 5 goal (verbatim): The user sees a believable Palm / Love / Career reading with the detected lines drawn over their own photo, saved on the device and shareable, built and verified against fixture data before the real backend is wired.

Note (MVP mode): the Goal line is not in user-story format and no story was invented.

Note (wave 2): runs beside 05-03, 05-04 and 05-05 on disjoint files and needs no physical phone. Instrumented tests seed readings through `ReadingEntryPoint` + `TestReadings.seedReading` (05-01) and never touch rows they did not create (RESEARCH Pitfall 10). Device commands set `ADB_SERIAL=emulator-5554` and go through `tools/device-lock.sh`.

<objective>
Turn the tracer's Result into the full S10 screen: smoothed, haloed lines that draw in once, a legend with its notes, the disclaimer above Palm / Love / Career tabs, a card per line with an "Approximate" or "Not clearly visible" badge, and every loading / missing-photo / unreadable state (RES-01, RES-02, RES-03, RES-04; D-07, D-08, D-09, D-10, D-11, D-12, D-13, D-24).

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 5 success criteria 1 (tabs, overlay with legend on three aspect ratios and every trace_source) and 2 (a card per line, disclaimer on every reading).
Output: OverlayMath and LineRules additions, the finished PalmPhoto, ResultScreen with tabs, ResultCards, and their JVM and instrumented tests.
</objective>
