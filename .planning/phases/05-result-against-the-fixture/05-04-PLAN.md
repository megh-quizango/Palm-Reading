## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 5 goal (verbatim): The user sees a believable Palm / Love / Career reading with the detected lines drawn over their own photo, saved on the device and shareable, built and verified against fixture data before the real backend is wired.

Note (MVP mode): the Goal line is not in user-story format and no story was invented.

Note (wave 2): runs beside 05-02, 05-03 and 05-05 on disjoint files. Everything runs on emulator-5554 (API 35, the LayerSnapshotV28 path). Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 5 success criterion 5 (share as a branded image card).
Output: ShareCard and ShareCapture, Share.kt helpers, the Share button in ResultActions, the FileProvider entry and paths, the PalmApp cleanup, and ShareTest.
</objective>
