## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 5 goal (verbatim): The user sees a believable Palm / Love / Career reading with the detected lines drawn over their own photo, saved on the device and shareable, built and verified against fixture data before the real backend is wired.

Note (MVP mode): the Goal line is not in user-story format and no story was invented.

Note (variants): D-06 lists 13 variants and all 13 are built exactly. One more, "Valid · long text" (summary and interpretations of 600+ characters), is added for the UI-SPEC long-text backstop (Result at font scale 2.0 in en and hi, share-card truncation); it changes none of the 13.

Note (wave 2): no physical phone, no camera. Device commands set `ADB_SERIAL=emulator-5554` and go through `tools/device-lock.sh`. The Hindi fixture is Claude-written and joins the existing "native-speaker review before release" item (STATE.md, D-06 of Phase 3).

<objective>
Make every Phase 5 state reachable on the emulator: the Hindi and invalid fixtures, the 13 D-06 variants (plus a long-text one) derived in code and guarded by a drift and schema test, the language default, and the full debug Fixture lab with aspect, skin tone, variant and "Delete all readings" (D-02, D-04, D-05, D-06, D-18, D-24).

Purpose: lets 05-06 walk every variant in en and hi on three aspect ratios without a camera, and proves the fixtures obey the frozen contract (supports RES-02, RES-03, READ-03).
Output: FixtureVariant, the two new fixtures, the variant seam twins, the full lab, result-open.sh options, FixturesTest and FixtureRepositoryTest, and the fixtures.dir test property.
</objective>
